Who We Are

The city's loudest
night out, x3 a year.

Bar Scene Socials started in 2019 with one green-soaked St. Paddy's crawl and a simple idea: Milwaukee's best nights happen between bars, not inside them. Today we throw three signature crawls a year, pack thousands of wristbands, and give a cut of every ticket back to the neighborhoods that host us.
